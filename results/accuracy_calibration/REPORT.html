EEG cutoff and delivered latency

The new comparison uses EEG only before +6.5 seconds after the attention trigger. Inference, chunk arrival and transport latency were not measured for these models. The six verified local streaming exports are earlier [2.5,3.5) s continuous-filter CSP models from a separate pipeline. That historical study did not verify a Riemannian streaming export. The separate current early-stream checks do not change its numerical results.

What this means for the rehab track

The four-second scoring window overlaps nominal feedback from approximately +3.5 s. Causal filtering prevents future-sample contamination but cannot remove cues, stimulation, visual feedback or artifacts already recorded. Three participants, six repeated sessions and absent per-trial feedback/clinical outcome labels do not establish recovery or therapeutic efficacy. The useful handoff is the measured calibration burden, poor transfer cells and a Riemannian candidate for independent evaluation.
